Accounts & cards

Add the last digits of each bank account or card. SMS, emails and statements are matched to the right one automatically (e.g. “A/c XX8810”, “Card *1279”). Tap an account to give it a short name like “Salary” or “Joint”.
Several numbers (e.g. account and debit card) can be separated with commas. Accounts found in imported statements, SMS and emails are added here automatically. Tap one to rename it.

🤖 DeepSeek AI (optional)

Not set up.
Kharcha uses DeepSeek only when its own rules can't: messages it doesn't recognise, statements in unknown formats (any bank's CSV, Excel or PDF), uncategorised entries, and questions in Summary → Ask. Get a key at platform.deepseek.com → API keys (pay-as-you-go; a request costs well under ₹1). The text being read is sent to DeepSeek (servers in China); your key stays on your devices.

Email (Gmail)

Not connected.
How to connect Gmail (one time, ~3 min)
  1. Tap Copy setup script below. It already contains your private key.
  2. Open script.google.com (signed in to the Gmail you get bank alerts on) → New project, delete what's there and paste.
  3. Deploy → New deployment → type Web app. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone. Tap Deploy and allow Gmail access (choose Advanced → Go to project if Google warns it's unverified — it's your own script).
  4. Copy the Web app URL and paste it in the box above.
The script runs inside your Google account and only answers requests carrying your key. It sends back just the short alert text around each transaction, nothing else from your mailbox.
